Post by sarf » 2003-02-05 17:38

bigmoose wrote:also i was wondering if there was any way to view how many slots a selected user was downloading from? its possible to view the uploads, so i dont see why it wouldnt be possible to view the downloads?

Because arnetheduck considered that information... irrelevant to have announced. I mean, who really cares how many downloads you have running? It doesn't say whether you are a cheater or not since you can have 3 upload slots and 43 download slots and still upload more than you download (unlikely but possible).
For your information, it is very simple to code, but would put a higher bandwidth load on the server as the myinfo would have to be updated more often for the value to be accurate (if we would use the <++ tag to show the info).

To answer your question in one sentence: No, there is no way to find out who downloads from whom.

One thing that would be useful would be for the clients to report to whom they had uploaded a certain amount of data. This way you could get statistics on how much each client uploads (and downloads) in your hub. A discussion about a modification to this effect can be found on this forum, here.
