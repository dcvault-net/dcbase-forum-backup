tabs windows and OS

Post by reeferd » 2004-11-14 14:48

Even though windows has it's window tabs in the bottom, i just felt that it would be more natural to have the window tabs at the top. Because you are in fact jumping between the top window pane. The bottom upload/download status field is more or less static. Therefore it is unnatural to have these tabs all the way down there. It should be at the top. And the tab you currently have selected should "be part of" the rest of the window.

I would just like to point out that Windows behaves in this way too, with the taskbar at the bottom of the screen.


Well. windows behaves in many ways. But DC++ Is not an OS.
If I should compare my idea to something, it would be the way you naviagte when you right click "my computer" and select properties. I think that is a very easy way to navigate between windows with tabs.

DC++ is probably closest to mirc in functionality so personally i think it makes sense to have the tabs where they are now, actually i quite like them there.


I have grown acustomed to having them at the bottom too, but it still is a bit 'daft' to separate those buttons from the window you are, in fact, changing. If you had any imagnination, you'd realize what I am trying to say here is a good idea. just kidding... hehe.

But I think it would be good useability to move those tabs. If not all the way to the top, then maybe over the upload/download field. Just connect the tab with the window.

(Always when I am evaluating the useability of a program, i start to think about how I reacted to the gui the first time I used it. I remember I was a little puzzled about the tabs, but I quickly realized that the upload/download panel was static.)

But this is no big deal. I think a bigger problems is the label the tab has. When you have 15 search windows, 5 hub windows and the downloadqueue and finished dl's open it becomes a bit messy. I like the suggestion shannon has about the look of the tabs. But I would also like to see a search icon for search windows, and maybe a hub icon for hubs etc. And since the tabs shrink a bit when you have many windows, the full label should be displayed when you hover over the tab. Now I refrain from opening too many windows. It becomes too messy. And it takes time to go through them all to close them.
