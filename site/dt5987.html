Post by GargoyleMT » 2003-11-02 15:36

The number of slots open and total is only sent to other clients with search replies.

In short, there's no easy way (without wasting hub bandwidth) to get the information you want.

Note: for Neo-Modus direct connect 2.x, the O: tag is free slots, while S: is total. That's the only semi-workaround I can think of.
