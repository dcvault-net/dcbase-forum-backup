Hi!:)

I just got an idea for a great new feature! So...like...I'm soon beginning to sort my MP3s to make my collections more organized. Then I thought that when I download in DC then I have to Browse for the specific folder if I want to put it there in the beginning. Now...since I'm very lazy to do this normally, I just download it to the default download directory and it's just piling up and everything's will go wrong again. So...I thought of a feature, that I could specify a list of folders, that should be displayed in the dl menu, so I don't have to browse for it...or even you could make a label for each dir. Like this:

Soundtracks: E:\Media\Music\Soundtracks
Albums: E:\Media\Music\Albums
Films: E:\Media\Films
etc.

And they could be appended to the 'Download here' menu devided with a seperator...:) It'd be cool:)
