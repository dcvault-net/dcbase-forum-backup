Free the slot every 100MB

Post by mai9 » 2003-06-18 01:54

I know there's an upload queue on works. but what about a feature that denies (for 10 seconds) the slot to a user that has downloaded 100MB (or whatever) in a row?

Would this be very complex to program? Maybe this is a quick and dirt feature only for mods?

Well, you clearly see that the object of this feature would be to distribute the slots to more people. It's not perfect, but theoretically DC++ only has to stockpile MB transfered for every user who is currently using a slot. When he makes 100MB, DC++ stops the user for 10 seconds, enough time for other users to claim that slot. If nobody claims it, the first user regains the slot and download continues.

I hope this idea didn't come up before in the countless threads on leeching

Post by jbyrd » 2003-06-18 20:44

This feature doesn't appeal to me...at all.

Take a 600mb movie on a 3 slot uploader, for example.

Just say that it takes 4 hours for me to download 100 mb. If I get in on the queue early and secure a slot for myself, I should be done in 1 day. If three other people were getting that same movie at the same speed, three different people have the download per day.

However, lets say that the 100mb restriction is put into play, and 6 people are alternating slots. I download for an hour, and someone else downloads for an hour. You do this enough, and you'll get 6 people getting the download in 2 days.

Now, the average in both cases is 3 people a day, but it takes me 2 days to download it instead of 1 day.

Since I jumped in on the queue first, I should get first service. That's how I feel.

Now, quickly, throw some slower connections into the picture, say half as fast. Now, I don't get mine until there's is done...4 DAYS. I could have downloaded it in 1 day. Throw some 56k's in there and you have some serious horse crap.

I think this feature would only hurt the plusplus community.
Please don't incorporate it into dc.
