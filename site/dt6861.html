Post by crack22 » 2003-12-09 21:05

first of all: hi@all !

i'm using dc++ for a while now but then i moved to another city (finally left fuckin home )

new city ment new ISP

good thing was: 10mbit for free (college)
problem was: proxy plus firewall (all ports filterd exept 21,80,8080, 1080)

i said good bye to counterstike and battle net (i'm too old for this stuff anyway ) and edonkey and dc++

but then i started looking for solutions...

i used http tunnel, nice but only tunnels tcp (no game, no leech)
and finally ended up on a site http://www.socks.permeo.com/..

i never heared of "socks" but worked me into the stuff and found out that u can forward/tunnel EVERY application trough port 1080 if socks5 protocol is on the proxy..

so i tried it and and tata... it worked fine..

i can use dc++ and a few games (UnrealTournament, global ops, ironstorm) and irc and ftp

you have to ensure that your proxy server has socks5 or socks4 protocol running. give it a try! it works fine

proof:download pic


if you need help, reply
