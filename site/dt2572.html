Post by GargoyleMT » 2003-05-15 04:21

Pensign, DC++ will pick from higher priority files from a user before low priority ones. If "lowest" is the highest priority on a file from a user, that's the only time it'll be picked. Are you sure you're seeing what you said you're seeing?

A solution to the second part of your post is to have a mini-priority scale (able to be turned on and off though a setting) that will give more complete files priority over others in the same priority band. (i.e. a 500kb completed normal download would have priority over 128kbyte normal download, and that would have it over an unstarted normal download). Nobody's made such a patch and submitted it to Arnetheduck for inclusion in DC++ as far as I know though.
