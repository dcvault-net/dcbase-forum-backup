Re: How to handle forced 24h-disconnection/downloads ?

Post by GargoyleMT » 2006-02-15 17:08

Dave06 wrote:The question is how to deal with that issue when I currently have a download running which probably will last 50 hours ?

What problems are you having?

If your ISP disconnects you every 24 hours, the transfer will be interrupted. And it's possible (or even probable) that the source will have other users waiting, and so you won't get a slot when you reconnect. There's nothing you can do about that.

If you spell out the root problem, we might be able to help.
