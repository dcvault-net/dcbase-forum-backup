Post by Gadget » 2003-05-18 19:27

New dynamic hublist at http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2 with over 2000 online hubs.
The hublist uses www.hublist.org database on real-time and it's customizable in many ways:

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?SE.bz2
gives list of all swedish hubs (choose any country)

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?anime.bz2
gives list of all anime hubs (choose any keyword)

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?users>100&.bz2
list of hubs with over 100 users

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?users<200&.bz2
list of hubs with under 200 users

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?share>100&.bz2
list of hubs with over 100 GB minimum share limit

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?share<20&.bz2
list of hubs with under 20 GB minimum share limit

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?share=10&.bz2
list of hubs with 10 GB minimum share limit

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?share=10-20&.bz2
list of hubs with minimum share limit between 10 GB and 20 GB

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?slots<3&.bz2
list of hubs with under 3 minimum slot limit

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?slots>5&.bz2
list of hubs with over 5 minimum slot limit

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?slots=1-10&.bz2
list of hubs with minimum slot limit between 1-10

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?port<>411&.bz2
list of hubs which are not in port 411

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?port>1024&.bz2
list of hubs which are in port above 1024

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?port=80&.bz2
list of hubs which are in port 80

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?country=fr&.bz2
list of hubs which are in france

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?country<>fr&.bz2
list of hubs which are not in france

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?search=mp3&.bz2
list of hubs which description, name or address contain the string "mp3"

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?nick=gadget&.bz2
list of hubs where is user whose nick contains "gadget" connected

http://www.hublist.org/PublicHubList.config.bz2?search=finx&country=fi&nick=gadget&users>10&share<100&.bz2
list of hubs whose description, name or address contains "finx", are located in Finland, there is an user whose nick contains "gadget", over 10 users are connected to the hub and minimum share is below 100 GB

Have fun!
