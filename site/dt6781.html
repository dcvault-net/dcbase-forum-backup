Post by TheParanoidOne » 2003-12-07 14:35

joakim_tosteberg wrote:mount them with a prog like daemon tools.

This is the better solution. It is a lot less fiddly and does not require you to use twice the disk space - once for the image and once for the extracted files. Extracting files also means that you miss out on things in the CD image such as CD audio data.
