Post by Xan1977 » 2004-06-11 05:02

Active mode requires you to enter your IP address in settings. A lot of people are assigned a new IP address by their ISP every time they reboot their broadband modems, or every time they reconnect to a dialup service. In order to avoid updating DC++ settings every time your IP address changes, a custom domain name from www.dynip.org (edit: www.dyndns.org , der ) or www.no-ip.com coupled with an automatic IP update program can be used. Enter the dyndns address, say joey.no-ip.net instead of the actual IP address and let the dyndns updater do the work for you.

So, which part are you having trouble with?
