Post by yakko » 2004-08-11 19:26

It happened again. I had a power outage and when my hub came up the plugin was enabled. This is my DnsBl.xml (it's disabled at the moment so unregistered people can use my hub):

Code: Select all

<DnsBl Enabled="0">

        <Commands MinLevel="4"/>
        <Paranoia>some</Paranoia>
        <AllowRegisteredUsers>yes</AllowRegisteredUsers>
        <LogFile></LogFile>
        <Server Suffix="cbl.abuseat.org">
                <Reject>127.0.0.2</Reject>
        </Server>

        <!-- list of IPs not to check (can be x.x.x.x or x.x.x.x-y.y.y.y or x.x.x.x/y) -->

        <Ranges>
                <Allow>127.0.0.1</Allow>
                <Allow>10.0.0.0/8</Allow>
                <Allow>172.16.0.0/12</Allow>
                <Allow>192.168.0.0/16</Allow>
        </Ranges>

</DnsBl>
