Re: restart d++ just because you change the settings....

Post by GargoyleMT » 2003-11-03 17:05

Jo.King wrote:I would just like to know why do I have to restart d++ when I change for example the amount of shares.


In the settings, the only options that it says that you should restart DC++ for are those on the appearance page. However, that's not entirely true. You just must close down each hub window and open them again for them to take.

In addition to the /refresh command, 0.300 and above have a File > Refresh File List command.

If you add a directory in the Sharing Settings, no refresh is necessary, that is automatic.
