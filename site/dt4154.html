Post by Menchi » 2003-07-15 00:28

I want to start a petition to get proper bandwidth management into DC++


Lack of bandwidth management has caused the DC community to become diseased. Fakeshares, slot blockers, auto-kickers, modem fishing, unpopular files, min slots, are all there because it is painful to share. I want to share, share longer, share popular files, kick less, and keep sane slots.

Why does DC++ need upload management?
- prevent people from using non-standard clients
- it is painful to share with lag/choke problems not fixed with the small send buffer
- encourage people to share (instead of the alternatives)
- unfair shares (less aggressive uploads are pushed out)
- ISP quota's

The real issue here is that ISP's have created a world where upload bandwidth is scarce. We can't fix that, but we can fix DC++.


requirements:

User set (auto detecting is trickier than it looks)
- no reliable way to determine the connection
- % of bandwidth may be needed for other (unknown) reasons
- only we can know what we need, and will soon learn it isn't that much
standard (no moded versions of DC++)
- Everyone should be allowed to share, having to search for modified DC++ clients only makes this harder
- "hacked" clients will be less common (no need to compile your own)
- a standard tag allows hub operators to filter easily
fair
- prevent a single connection from taking over
- dealing with 56k connections
- "wasted" bandwidth should be kept to a minimum
Smooth
- ping times should not be affected
- makes online games possible (usually needs only 5k & a good ping)


myths:

its hard to do - it has been done
- the patch has been submitted to SF before
- Source is available everywhere

alternative uploading will kill dc - There are things that many consider more important than uploading on dc, are they? Should DC++ decide for you?
- servers; DC Hubs, ftp, games
- other p2p programs
- shared connections
