Actually, this suggestion has some merit to it, but in a more generic way. DC++ should perhaps remember all settings you apply to individual windows and restore them as appropriate.

Despite the comments about "one whole checkbox" and "a drop-down menu", there are in fact seven sections of the search window alone that you may wish to change. If you regularly search for similar things across sessions that take advantage of a number of these, then it would certainly be a benefit to have them "remembered".
