well, you can do this, or if you don't want to loose your settings, you can use the "manual way": close DC++, find DCPlusPlus.xml which is in your DC++ directory, open it in a text editor program (like Windows' Notepad), find the lines beginning with "SearchFrameOrder" and "SearchFrameWidths" (they should be in the beginning, for me they are lines 11 and 12) and remove these 2 lines.
on the next DC++ start, DC++ should reset these 2 values to the default ones.
