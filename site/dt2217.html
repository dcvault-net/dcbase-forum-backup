Post by GargoyleMT » 2003-04-17 15:54

Sammas, first, are you aware that you're posting in the Developer's Help topic, which is devoted to helping people understand the DC++ source code?

It looks like you're asking about upload queueing in DC++. There is no system for this currently, and I believe that operators and registered users are not any better than regular users, and I would not like them to have prefferential treatment when downloading from me (except for OPs getting my file list for checking against the hub rules). Priority by share size is also very naieve.

If this is really what you're asking about, search the archives for "Ratings System" - it is a long thread, but Sarf and Volkris (and others) have been pretty thorough about thinking through the consequences of various schemes.
