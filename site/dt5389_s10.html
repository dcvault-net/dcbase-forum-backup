Post by arnetheduck » 2003-10-07 00:07

On ghosts:
DCH++ tries to send data for 30 seconds to someone who's scheduled for disconnect (to allow kick messages to get through)...this is the max lifetime for a ghost in theory, so that's why the ghosts disappear after a short while...I'll lower this in the next release so it shouldn't be as noticable...
In practice, I suspect a bug, specially in the linux version that under strange circumstances keep the ghosts online anyway...I'm looking into it though...
