Post by mcqfesijiba » 2003-11-27 23:39

This may not be exactly what you want, but if you have TV out on your video card, you can have a movie player such as BSplayer play movies directly on your TV. Then there's no need for a separate player or conversion to VCD/SVCD. That method works for video only. You can use a good sound card to then output the sound to your TV or to good speakers.

It's what I do and much less hassle, except for the fact that my AV cable barely reaches my TV.
