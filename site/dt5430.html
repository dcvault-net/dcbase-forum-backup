re: hublist:
I notices you timed out on my list.. 30 seconds was not enough for the pinger to get the whole bunch of myinfo's it seems (so you were reported as having less users)

re: dreamland vs vandel:
you're not the only one having problems with dreamland. personally I haven't seen it's pinger around for a couple of days. vandel hasn't shown up either (I started watching all pingers 5 days ago).
the problem is not the same as with vandel. vandel's problem is that it doesn't send any MyINFO (or at least, it didn't when I checked), and without myinfo, the pinger won't get any nicklists which it uses to check your hub.

re: lag:
you could try to disable the WriteTimeout in dchpp.xml, dunno if that helps

re: time-consuming login:
I'll ask arne to add a "<DCH++> sending userinfo... please wait" ... "<DCH++> done" in there, as it feels a bit odd to sit and wait there while it's seemingly doing nothing (this could also be fixed by a plugin or something)
