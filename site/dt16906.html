Svin wrote:I think I need an explanation to what TTH root actually is...


In simple words : TTH its a hashing algorythm just like MD5 or the others you mentioned. TTH Root is the hash key (same as MD5 key in MD5)

For more info go to : http://ullner.blogspot.com/2006/03/tige ... html#links TTH is briefly covered there.

So I used a program called Express checksum calculator, and all three had the same checksum (CRC32, MD5, SHA-1).

Then you dont have small chance of disk problems and the files are surely identical.
