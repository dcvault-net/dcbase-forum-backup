2 new plugins are comming up in the next release of DSHub when its out

Lookup - A Network Admin Tool

This plugin is for those who operate the hub with powerful tools like:
  • whois
  • traceroute
  • revdns
  • ping
RSS - The News Bringer
This tool reads RSS feeds from homepages and presents them into the client.

Both plugins are available for testing under plugin sections as beta product.
