Hi,
I am quite new in DC++. My operating system is Windows Xp Home Addition and the version of my DC++ is 0.691.
1) In my "Sharing" I have, so far, included 2 folders, each of which consists of various MP3 files. So, it is not important for a user to know, which songs are included in each of these folders. He simply inserts in the "Search" tab the name of the song he is looking for, and if that song is included in one of those two folders, he woud get it. Therefore, in the "Description" tab of my my Personal Information, I wrote just "Audio - various sorts". Still, in the Settings of "Sharing" there appears a note : "Files appear in the share only after they've been hashed". Could you, please, explain what does this mean ?

2) I would like to add to my "Sharing" a few additional folders, each of which would consist of the contents (in wav format) of a part of a CD. Hence, in this case it is important for the user to know what is the content of each such folder. Could you, please' guide me, how can I make the users know it ?
Thanks in advance for your help.
