The idea is this: if you help someone who is infringing copyrights and you know it, it provides legal grounds for you to become at least partially responsible for his infringement. We don't want that.

Read the MGM vs. Grokster court decision for more guidelines, but that's one of the big ones. It's what the EFF paper for P2P developers says too.
