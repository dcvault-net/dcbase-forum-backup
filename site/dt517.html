Post by aDe » 2003-01-29 23:11

some of the smaller creations i made...

Applications..
http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/FavHubManager-v2.exe
With this you can import your favourite hubs from NMDC to DC++.

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/GLM-TraxSender-v5.exe
This tool sends the track title you are currently listening to to the dc main chat. Works with nmdc & dc++, winamp 2.8 & 3, foobar2k, mmjukebox, zoomplayer.

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/FindDCHub.exe
Find the nmdc hub window when you loose that little tray icon.

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/DCHub_DreamReg.zip
A standard version 1.00.25 of the nmdc hub that registers to the dreamland hublist server.

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/GLMp3Alarm.exe
Not really dc related. Executes a playlist at a given time..

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/RedirectHub-v1.zip
A tiny 'hub' that only redirects clients. Optionally sends a PM/chat msg.

Scripts...
http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/IPBlocker.zip
Blocks IP ranges from baaad companies..

http://phats0.d2g.com/dctools/GLMLDonkeyfilter2.zip
Blocks mldonkeys by name/sharesize

enjoy
