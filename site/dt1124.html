DC Hub in XP

Post by ukmovieland » 2003-03-07 18:06


Yes it is possible to create a DC Hub in XP. You need the DChubsetup from NeoModus, and the Client for DC

I suggest you browse to www.dyndns.org first to create an account that can give you a DNS address (e.g yourname@serveftp.org) so that you do not have to give out your IP address for users to sign in.
