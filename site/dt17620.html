how can I see the list of all possible hubs in the world that I can connect to??
Go to the website www.hublist.org , they list every hub. They also have information on how to get the 'full' hub list to use in your client.
Can I add that list to DC++ program in " manual connect address"??
Manual connect address is to connect to individual hub addresses you have, the two things are unrelated. But yes, you can add the full list to your DC++ client.
Now, my second concern. I want to connect to hubs of Latin America, most important from Venezuela, Columbia, Mexico, Peru and Argentina. Because of my strange version of the program, there isn't shown which hub from which country is as it normally should, so I have no ideas to which hubs I am connecting.
