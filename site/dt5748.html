* If DC++ receives "banned" during the login phase, it'll stop automatically reconnecting (nÃƒÂ¶jd nu, nev? =) [happy now, nev?]

this 'feature' was implemented for a reason. when you get kicked/banned for a couple of minutes, the reason - in most cases - is that there is something wrong with your share. the OPs do not want you gone, but they want you to fix your share.
now, when you are not around, you cannot fix your share, can you? why the fsck should you reconnect then?
Nev, among others have argued to implement this feature because the usual scenario was this:
- user X has bad files
- user X gets kicked for an n amount of minutes, to give him/her time to fix the share
- user X is not around, user X reconnects WITH THE SAME CRAP IN HIS SHARE
- user X gets permanently banned, because he/she hasn't fixed their share.

I have no clue what you mean by "your hip is not the same", but I don't think it outweighs the advantage of the way it works now.

OPs cannot "know" if the user refuses to fix their share, or that the user simply is away.
live with this situation, as it is way better than it was before.
