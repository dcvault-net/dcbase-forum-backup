hi there

I noticed that its possible to transfere the queue from dc to dc++, by doin it manually (cut and paste) only thing that I dont get right is the date... since dc dont seem to have that

its a bit of hard work, but doable, if you have a really big queue it will take some time, and dc++ must be closed during it (or at least be shut down before the items shows in queue)

a queued item from dc looks like this:
[BBB]mickisthelady 0|0|4345103|D:\Mina dokument\Direct Connect Downloads\Downloading\Madonna - Love Makes The World Go Round.mp3|musik\album\madonna\Madonna - Love Makes The World Go Round.mp3|[BBB]mickisthelady|

when pasting it in dc++ queue it would be like this:
<Download Target="D:\Mina dokument\Direct Connect Downloads\Downloading\Madonna - Love Makes The World Go Round.mp3" Size="4345103" Priority="3" TempTarget="" Added="1083704530">
<Source Nick="[BBB]mickisthelady" Path="musik\album\madonna\Madonna - Love Makes The World Go Round.mp3" Utf8="0"/>
</Download>

note: to find the queuefile from dc you need to use show hidden files
