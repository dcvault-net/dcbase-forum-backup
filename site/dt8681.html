Re: DC++ on Mac OS 9

Post by GargoyleMT » 2004-03-12 02:11

Ekaqu wrote: I was wondering if you guys could make a version of DC++ that worked on OS 9.

This is no small thing you ask for. It would:
a) suck, since my only OS9 running mac is sitting, partially gutted, in my closet.
b) take a long time, both to replicate the DC+ gui on whatever toolkits the mac provides for windowing (but mostly learning the platform)
c) require a lot of motivation to be completed,
d) be a waste of effort, since OS9 has no future.

There is a team (who I haven't had personal contact with, but would love to hear from) porting DC++ to OS X: http://sourceforge.net/projects/macdcpp/

You're free to run VirtualPC and run DC++ under that. It may, sadly, be the only way you'll get a DC client running on your mac.
