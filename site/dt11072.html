Bugzilla bugs policy

Post by arnetheduck » 2004-06-27 13:53

So, we have 5 votes until an item is tagged as "new"...you don't have to touch them until that happens, but if you see anything interesting or manage to confirm the bug yourselves you're welcome to confirm it by hand.

The only crash reports I want in the bugzilla are the ones that are 100% reproducible, i e "crash when click here" or "crash when loading filelist, filelist attached"...the rest will go to the automatic crash report tool that's still under development...

Feature requests also go to the bugzilla, with status "enhancement"...the same goes for rejected features, I still have to make a "rejected features" link somewhere that automatically searches the bugzilla for rejects...

I'll put a few markers in the old trackers, anything past those markers is to be deleted. simple and effect way to teach the bastards to read =)

comments? suggestions?
