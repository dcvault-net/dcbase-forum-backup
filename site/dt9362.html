Guitarm wrote:Can somebody please explain why the word parts is used instead of leaves?, and the difference between them?

Parting, Joining are two words that are commonly used in IRC clients to describe the act of leaving and coming into a channel. If arne was the first person to use those words in his client, it's probably because DC bears such a similarity to IRC on the surface.
