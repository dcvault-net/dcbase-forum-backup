Post by Crack1 » 2003-03-23 00:07

Well so did I and here is what I learned in the last hour.

The research - I've used DC++ for a few months. Other then that I'm a newb to this whole file-sharing biz.

Start up - umm well I really didn't do anything. I have an old fileserver with 6 scsi drive that my work just gave me. So I decided to put it to some use outa my house.

The Software - yeah again I really didn't have to think to much about this one. I needed something that was quick and easy to install, oh and that works on WinXP. So I chose Neo-modus at http://www.neo-modus.com/?page=Download This hub software required the client be installed, no biggie for me since I had it already.

Installation/Setup - I just clicked the install file that installed the hub software. After launching the software I set up my IP and a brief description. Set users to max, set myself up as an op and click "Start Serving".

Scripts - Again I have no experiance in anything here so I browsed this forum for advice. Found a sit that had DC++ scripts and I downloaded 2. One that tells jokes evey few minutes and one that tells the time every 15 minutes. At the scripts tag on the DC++ software I clicked "new" and just copied and pasted the downloaded scripts into them. Now I've already logged into my hub and the scripts just started to work. -- cool =)

Now I have no idea if anyone else can log into my hub or not. I havn't seens anyone else in it but me.

Oh and another quick piece of info that I picked up. Your Hub will not regester with the public list for 24 hours(Twenty Four) so don't wig out when you don't see your hub on the list right away.

Well that is my first hour on hub creation. For other newbs trying to start a hub I hope this will help a bit.

So where am I now....

Post by Crack1 » 2003-04-03 19:36

Well its been a few weeks so I thought I would throw up an update as to where I am at and a few things I have learned along the way.

First let me say I've revamped my hub a few time. I think i got it right now though. At first I installed YAMPs but YAMBs take out the part of the hub were you can put in new scripts and edit them right from the hub program itself. So I deleted this and started over. I meet up with "Snooze" and he gave me a link to his site. He had a general bot script there that I used. It works great. It does everything I need. I also installed a small trivia bot that is kinda fun.

Second thing I did was install a router on my home network. No I ran into some issues with this. Being that I'm behind a router that is NATing out an IP range to all my computer on the network I needed to forward a port to my hub. As well as DC++ which I was using at the time. For each computer that had DC++ on I had to setup on port forwarding with in the router setup. I also had to set a port (I choose 411) for my hub to talk through. Now people trying to connect through my router are sent where they need to go.

Third thing I did was mask my IP via no-ip.com. The was a free thing to do and I though was kinda cool.

Thats it now my hub is up and running. I got all my friends there and we are having a blast sharing our stuff.

Thanks to everyone for there help - feel free to stop by and say hi anytime.
