Firstly, kazaa is VERY easily cheated. Heck, I do it, and I don't cheat in any of the other things. Truth is, I didn't have to. I use kazaap not for the cheating thing, but for the other options. For some unknown reason I got the full participation level when I had nothing shared in normal kazaa. Not a very good system if you ask me.

Now, as to why it would help people cheat and leech too much if you let them have the prioritizing system, it's actually kind of simple. All a cheater or leecher has to do is give higher priorities to 56k users and things like that so that no one with the faster connections can get in to them and get their bandwidth. The fact is that it would only help when some person serving actively watches it pretty often, and most people aren't going to do that.
