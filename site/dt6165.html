Post by HaArD » 2003-11-12 01:01

Tas,

DC:Pro uses F: for bandwidth limiting, "F:<DL limit>/<UL limit>"

eg Tag: <DC:PRO V:0.2.2.11,M:A,H:0/1/2,S:6,F:120/30>

The Download limit in DC:Pro is based on the UL Limit to a maximum of:

UL Limit of 1 to 5 kb/s = 2x for DL
UL Limit of 6 to 10 kb/s = 3x for DL
UL Limit of 11 kb/s and up = 4x for DL
