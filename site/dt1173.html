Post by mo » 2003-03-01 15:38

I don't like the alt nick idea for 2 reasons.
1) if your nick is that common, it's hard to queue something from that person and get it at a later time if they disconnect mid transfer.
2) if you have an alt-nick, you could be online and people who have things queue'd from you would have to re-queue everything from you again...

a simple reconnect after x time is the best way to resolve this for the community.

On a side note, it would be nice if there was some additional information saved about the user your downloading a file from. (connection speed, dc client, email, descritpion, etc..)

If at some later date you try and dl from this person and get a "file not found" error, this information could be compared before the username is removed from the list of valid people to download the file from.

just a thought...
