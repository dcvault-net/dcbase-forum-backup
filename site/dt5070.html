vandel405.dynip.com is in there by default (hub setting) it is for registering a hub to a hub list.

Dr.Hoffmann did you actualy clic on "start serving" button ?

in DC connect to your hub by entering " 127.0.0.1:411 " in "manual connect address".(use what is show in "listening port" in hub setting)
in this case 411 must be the port show in "listening port" in hub setting.

in hub setting "my IP address" must be the IP of puter that the hub is running on, or DNS addy like yournameaddy.no-ip.com if you want others to be able to connect to your hub. (need to register an account with them before using it) .
