Post by Guitarm » 2004-09-24 00:54

Amazing...

I have received a few private messages from someone. You probably know who it is. He writes his questions using 2 instead of two/to/too for example.

I told him that if he wanted any help he'd better write in plain, understandable english.

But this is the thing, In his last mail he actually tries to say, (and I'm translating now..), "Ok, I'll write in plain, understandable english, is this ok?".

What I've come to understand is - The poor guy doesn't even understand howto write plain, simple english. He actually thinks that his use of this.....(I don't know what to call it) language IS plain english.

This is not to pick on peoples spelling or anything, and it is absolutely an off-topic issue. I just wanted to express my thoughts regarding some of the "youth" asking things in the forum.
