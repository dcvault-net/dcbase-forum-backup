Supporting non-DC++ clients?

Post by TheParanoidOne » 2004-07-25 14:15

There seem to be more and more people who are coming and asking for help with clients that are not DC++ (or maybe I'm just noticing this more). I'm not sure what the "official" take on this should be. Personally I'd prefer to say "We can't help you with anything other than DC++" and leave it at that.

This also applies to Programmers Help, with people compiling non-DC++ source. There is a guy there who is asking general modification questions but it is clear that he is using CZDC++ source. Should he be told the "We only support DC++" line too?

Thoughts?

Note: I know that BCDC++ is a special case and should probably be exempt from some of the above.
