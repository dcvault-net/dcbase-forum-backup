Post by Gasman1015 » 2004-05-21 06:53

An image will fit on one cd because it is an image of one disk.

However when you are converting xvid avi's to vcd or svcd the file size will almost double
depending on the quality you want, any conversion will deteriate the existing quality.
You can use a program such as Guitarm suggested, (TMPGenc) to convert these files and chop them to specific sizes, it can also be done by altering the bitrate of the movie, but with further deteriation.
You will need to study the guides as suggested by Xan to learn exactly as their are many other factors you may need to consider, it is not a topic that can be done holding your hand.
Always remember you're unique, just like everyone else.
