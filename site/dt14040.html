The traffic was earlyer used to spam hub wide with searches, now when a match is found it grabs the list, hoping to save on searches... so its give some, to lose some. Traffic that is.

For some (like me?) its a good feature, where as people getting random files only, might have less use for it.. i generaly handle spanned archives, meaning that if theres 1 search hit, there is likely to be from 25-a few 100 more hits, of the same user. Guys getting "random" stuff, like movies/games, might only save a search, or two, depending on exacly what they are getting, and then it might be a "waste" (guys getting mpeg's for a 2 cd svcd might suffer for example.. the filelist uppload takes more bandwidth then the search for one extra file - however, it still saves the hubsoft the passive searches bandwidth usage, and all the other users replying bandwidth.. )

Prolly someone will come along with a more 'sientific' response if you ask for it, but i think i coverd most important parts with my ramblings.. and offcourse, if you dont like the feature, tell your friends about it, and have a mutual agreement to shut it off.. it would leave your downloads off one and other a little faster aswell, perhaps... (since your nolonger upploading fileists all the time - aswell as UDP (searches) dosent stand still, waiting for a response, its a bit more bandwidth echonomical, but less 'safe')

Best of luck.
