Hmm, you already know it's MPEG2 and haven't figured out you need an MPEG2 codec? (a DVD-program will play the file, btw, for example PowerDVD). Generally speaking I tend to stay away from codec-packs, but FFDShow is the one I would use if I had to.
2004-0725 is the latest version of that.

I personally use separate codecs: divx, xvid, mpeg2 + ac3 for audio and have yet to come across a file that wouldn't work on my system.
