That's a quite common problem with s-video -> composite. You need to get better cables that merges the two "image-channels" in the s-video into a single one in the composite cable. At the moment your cable only connects the black/white-part, not the colour. (I had to buy a couple of cables/connectors before I found some that worked.)

Is it PAl or NTSC?

Post by Dragula Aeop » 2004-08-31 01:54

Another thing to do is if you have a dvd drive and a dvd movie try playing it in your computer and seeing if it still comes out black+white on your tv. Make sure that on the dvd it says NTSC if you are in the USA, or PAL if you are in Europe/Asia. If you are in the USA and you play a NTSC movie in the drive and it still comes out bw, then it is probably your cable, as mentioned earlier. But if it comes out fine, then the problem is that the dvd-rips and/or cams you downloaded are in PAL format which is incompatible with NTSC tvs, unless you have an in-line converter, but is perfectly compatible with your computer. If it turns out that the dvd plays fine, try re-encoding it. If it still doesn't work then re-post.
