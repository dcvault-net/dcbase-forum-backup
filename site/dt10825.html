CDEx? I've heard of it, but I have no reason to use anything other than Exact Audio Copy.

It supports CDDB (is there a CD ripper that doesn't?) that is an easy automatic (assuming someone's entered the data for you) way of getting artist/song names.


If you don't want to be bogged down in these details, iTunes has a nice intuitive interface, and it does have a secure ripping mode. The encoder isn't LAME, so most people won't prefer it, but it'll get you by.
