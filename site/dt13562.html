Post by ullner » 2004-10-31 12:31

I would like to clarify: you can insert multiple hublists. Seperate them with a ; (semi colon). They will unfortunatly not be used at the same time due to the fact that DC++ don't store the hublists locally.
Example: hublist1.bz2;hublist2.bz2;hublist3.bz2 (in settings)
If you don't get hublist1.bz2, DC++ will move on to the next which is hublist2.bz2 and if that is unavailable (because of some reason), DC++ will move on the the next which is hublist3.bz2 and will continue to move on until DC++ finds a hublist that get downloaded.
