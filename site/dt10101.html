Post by Sedulus » 2004-05-09 17:41

won't happen
read up on the ClientVersion thread.. it speaks about why client types and versions are not interesting/important, while features are (read: _features_ not _quirks_)
furthermore, you may think that the features you want are simple. but they require not only quite a bit of work (implementing all kinds of checks along every protocol negotiation, extremely bloating the application), they also need lots of maintainance (adding the peculiarities of different versions of different apps when they come out)

if the client tag does not provide version info (by means of the tag in the description), the client does not want to be detected. if the mod programmers are too stupid to implement dc++-like behaviour correctly when claiming they are dc++ (for instance) that's just sad, but not something you should rely on.
